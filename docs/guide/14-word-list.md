# 14. Word list

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

What the computer words in this guide mean, in plain English.

**Address bar**: The long box at the top of the browser that shows the web address of the page you're on.

**Browser**: The program you use to visit websites, like Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

**Detection**: The setting in a rule that says how to compare its URL Fragment to a page's address: Contains, Starts with, Ends with, Exact or Regex. See [Choosing which pages a rule covers](05-which-pages-a-rule-covers.md).

**Domain**: A website's name, like `google.com`. A domain can have several sites under it, like `mail.google.com` and `docs.google.com`.

**Extension**: A small add-on program for your browser. Tab Automator is an extension.

**Favicon**: The small picture on the left side of a tab, usually the website's logo.

**FQDN** (fully qualified domain name): A website's full name, including the part in front, like `mail.google.com`.

**Group** (tab group): A set of tabs Chrome keeps together, with a colored label above them.

**Pinned tab**: A tab shrunk down to just its picture, kept at the far left of your tabs.

**Regex** (regular expression): A short way to write a text pattern. In Tab Automator you mostly use it to list several websites in one rule, like `bbc\.com|cnn\.com`. The `|` means "or," and `\.` means a real dot.

**Rule**: Your instructions to Tab Automator: "when I open a page from *this website*, do *these things* to its tab."

**Session**: A saved set of tabs you can open again all at once.

**Side panel**: The panel that opens on the right side of the browser when you click the Tab Automator button.

**Tab**: Each page you have open in the browser has a tab along the top of the window, showing its name and picture.

**Tab Hive**: The Tab Automator feature that closes tabs you haven't used in a while and keeps a list of them so you can bring them back.

**TLD** (top-level domain): The ending of a website's name, like `.com`, `.org`, `.gov` or `.co.uk`.

**Toolbar**: The row of buttons to the right of the address bar.

**URL**: A web address, like `https://www.example.com/recipes/soup`.

**URL Fragment**: In a rule, the piece of a web address Tab Automator looks for, like `example.com`.

**Workspace**: In Tab Automator, any open tab group. The Workspaces page lets you move a group to its own window, save it, or close it.
