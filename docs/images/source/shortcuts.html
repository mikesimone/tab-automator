Four shortcuts worth remembering

Change any of them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts
Alt+Shift+M

Move to a new window new

Sends the current tab's whole group (or just the tab, if it isn't in a group) to a new window. Perfect for demos.

Alt+Shift+P

Pause or resume all rules new

Turns every rule off at once, then back on. The toolbar icon shows a pause mark while rules are paused.

Alt+Shift+E

Spot Search

Search your open tabs and bookmarks from any page, then jump straight to the one you want.

Alt+Shift+W

Merge all windows

Pulls the tabs from all your browser windows into the one you are in.

On a Mac, Alt is the Option key.
